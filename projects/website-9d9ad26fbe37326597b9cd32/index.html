12 engine innovations that make every show unique

new

🎮 Virtual Console

Design your own control surface with buttons, sliders, and frames. Drag-and-drop layout editor with snap-to-grid. Flash triggers, toggles, chaser loops, scene recalls, and song presets — all mapped to DMX in real time. Saved inside your project file.

core

🎛️ AI Auto-Script

Drop any audio file and Light Maestro generates a complete DMX show — colors, movements, strobes, dimmers, and blackouts — synced to the music structure in seconds.

core

🎨 Dynamic Palette

Colors adapt to energy level — saturated and warm on drops, cool and desaturated on intros. Each genre brings its own hue-shift signature.

new

🔄 Smooth Crossfade

150ms micro-blend between adjacent cues — no sudden DMX jumps. Quadratic ease-in curve for natural transitions.

new

🌙 Progressive Fade Out

1-second quadratic ease-out at song end, 200ms on pause. DMX channels dim naturally — no hard cuts.

new

💡 Intelligent Strobe

Speed adapts to section energy — 1.5× on drops, 0.5× on intros. Soft sinusoidal dimmer pulse mode for fixtures with dimmer channels.

new

🎯 Parametric Movement

Pan/tilt amplitude and speed blend smoothly across sections. 11 movement patterns: sweep, circle, figure-8, spiral, zigzag, heartbeat, and more.

new

⚡ DJ Live Controls

Freeze holds the current frame indefinitely. Drop Now forces max energy + strobe for 300ms. One-click, no menu diving.

new

👁️ Dry-Run Preview

Toggle DRY mode to preview cues in 3D without sending to hardware. Same DMX computed, zero output — safe for on-the-fly experimentation.

new

📊 Waveform + Cue Overlay

Energy curve visualized above the AutoScript track. Cue markers color-coded by section — drop (red), chorus (orange), build (cyan), verse (green).

new

🆚 A/B Style Comparison

Generate two cue sets with contrasting styles (Techno ↔ House). Toggle between them live — compare before you commit.

new

🧠 Auto Section Detection

Energy curve peak detection identifies drops, verses, builds, and choruses — no manual markers needed. Falls back to time-based partitioning.

new

💾 Export Presets

Save complete shows as .lmpreset files — all cues, tracks, analysis data, and audio embedded. Share or archive your best work.
