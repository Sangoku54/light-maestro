Release notes — v1.0.43

  • Duplicate fixture: right-click → Duplicate — auto-places copy at the first free DMX slot after the source
  • Bar span control: adjustable support width (1–12 m) now defaults to all bar supports
  • Persistent musician positions: dragging a musician on the 3D stage keeps the custom position across page switches and project reopen
  • No ghost thumbnails: full cleanup of fixture groups/sprites on unpatch and unmount
  • unpatchFixture: correctly removes associated fixturePositions entry
  • headingRadForPos: atan2 formula fixed for correct default beam orientation
  • panTiltRead null: zone positions no longer overwritten when no DMX has been written
