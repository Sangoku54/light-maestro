Perform — Virtual Console & control

Custom virtual console: buttons, sliders, Group Masters (per-group submaster fader — caps the group dimmer while content stays dynamic), frames — drag & drop, pages & frames. MIDI Learn + LED feedback, MIDI Clock IN (BPM follows Ableton/Triton, 24 PPQ, hands back on stop), OSC (TouchOSC/Lemur), MTC timecode, LAN Web Remote (HTTP + state push).

  • Total MIDI mapping — every action is mappable
  • MIDI Clock IN: DAW tempo sync, zero config
  • Group submasters: the look comes down, dynamics stay alive
  • Web Remote: control from phone/tablet on the same LAN
  • Master Clock, real-time audio→DMX latency
