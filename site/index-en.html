Multi-universe DMX engine

USB (Enttec Open/Pro, DMXKing) — plug & play, dongle auto-detected at launch, ArtNet, sACN E1.31 — 1 universe in Standard, unlimited in Full. HTP/LTP merge, 60 fps, instant Grand Master & Blackout. Tour-stable.

🎚️

Audio Intelligence

Onset detection + adaptive BPM, frequency-band splitting, section detection, 8 audio-reactive effects. The music drives the lighting — not the other way around. — with guaranteed movement harmony: identical heads move together, the wave sweeps the rig.

💡

1500+ fixtures

Universal library. .qxf import, multi-modes, multi-heads. Chauvet, ADJ, BeamZ, Eurolite… and your customs in 2 minutes.

🎨

Complete show programming

Scenes, chasers, cue lists, RGB Matrix, EFX. Solo, group, universe — everything is addressable, everything is timed.

🕹️

Live Control

Virtual Console (buttons/sliders/frames) + MIDI Learn + OSC + MTC + LAN Web Remote. Your controllers light up in return. Your show, your hands.

🧱

Real-time 3D Stage

Realistic beams — fade along the beam, exact ground cutoff, projected gobos, real mechanical ranges (540°… pan) — the 3D shows what your fixtures will do, before rigging anything.

Field compatibility: ArtNet & sACN over the network, USB DMX direct, simultaneous multi-universe output. HTP/LTP, external merge, and a DMX log for debugging — it's all there.
Workflow

From audio to show, in 3 minutes.

  1. Import your track (MP3/WAV/FLAC) — automatic analysis.
  2. Let Show Auto generate chasers, colors, strobes, dimmer & EFX on your fixtures.
  3. Refine in Design (scenes/cues) then play in Perform (pads, sliders, MIDI).
